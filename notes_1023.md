### Expert Blind Spots

- while working with AI, humans can act as leaders and AI as workers.
- Can we measure the knowledge-transfer gap in both directions (human → AI and AI → human)?
- Can we develop a framework to study mutual expert blind spots and how they affect knowledge transfer?

### Reverse Study — [KITE](https://arxiv.org/pdf/2506.05579)

- look into the reverse, measure knowledge transfer from humans to AI during human–AI work.
- Humans learn through examples and practice, but different types of knowledge transfer depending on the task.
  - **Same domain, new problem:** We can reuse factual knowledge and previously learned strategies.
  - **Different domain:** Factual knowledge may not transfer, but other knowledge, such as writing style, structure, formatting, and wording, can still be useful.
- For example, someone who has written a research paper in one domain may later write a paper in another. While the subject knowledge changes, they can still apply what they learned about writing a paper.

**Research Questions**

1. Can we measure this type of knowledge transfer?
2. Can we transfer this to AI so it learns how humans improve and potentially adapts its architecture?
